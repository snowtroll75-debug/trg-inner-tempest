# Inner Tempest 0.10.0: Block 9, armour, magic items and aging

26 October 2026.

Needs **TRG Prime 3.673.0**. Install that first. On an older system the module warns at start-up, and the new runes, Uses and aging are not read.

Built to Graham's rulings of 26 October (B9-1 to B9-11 and interpretations 1–8, `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_RULINGS_2026-10-26-B9.md`; every proposal adopted) and the Block 9 audit (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK9_AUDIT.md`).

## What is new

Five new packs, each beside the system's own, so the pickers, the rune chooser and the shop find them:

| Pack | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Inner Tempest: Armour** | Chapter 6's nine pieces: Haramaki, Shozoku, Kikko, Do-maru, Kusari, Tatami-Do, Yoroi, Oni-Mask and Jingasa |
| **Inner Tempest: Runes** | Chapter 9's eleven runes, every tier (19 items). Centred is printed for armour and for weapons at two prices, so it is two items per tier: "Centred I (Armour)", "Centred I (Weapon)" |
| **Inner Tempest: Specific Magic Armour & Weapons** | The Harmonious Do-maru and the Spiritblade |
| **Inner Tempest: Wondrous Items** | Enlightened Prayer Beads, Lotus Blossom, Monastic Robe of Resilience, Mystic Incense Burner, Restorative Mat, Sash of Serenity |
| **Inner Tempest: Gear** | Incense, for the burner |

Each item prints the book's text, read from the docx, and then what the system does with it.

Also new:
- **Monastic Items.** A character with Inner Discipline may make a magic item through Meditation.
- **The Appendix's aging rules.** On by default.

## How they work

### The armour

| Armour | What the system does |
|---|---|
| **All nine** | The table's figures, read as the core's armour is: category, ACP, Max Dex, DR, HP, Speed, weight and traits. Full covers the arms and legs; Helm and the two masks or hats cover the head. |
| **Do-maru** | Full, but not the arms (B9-1). It covers the torso and legs, so a critical to the arms (where the shoulders are) reads the unarmoured row. The Harmonious Do-maru is the same. |
| **Shozoku** | +2 circumstance is offered on Hide (Stealth) while worn. Tick it in shadowy areas (interpretation 1). |
| **Oni-Mask** | +1 item is offered on Intimidate while worn. Tick it when the intimidation is visual (interpretation 1). |
| **Yoroi** | Its Helm takes the core's −1 to Perception. The Oni-Mask and the Jingasa print no Perception penalty and take none. |

### The runes

| Rune | What the system does |
|---|---|
| **Centred I–III** (armour or weapon) | +1 / +2 / +3 item bonus on every Refocus, taken automatically. Armour and weapon do not stack (both are item bonuses). |
| **Guarded** | When a critical hit lands on you, your player is asked before the damage is rolled (a reaction, once a day). On a yes: the critical's extra dice are rolled but not dealt, and you heal half of what they rolled, rounded up. |
| **Harmony** (armour or shield) | Press it (Engage, 1 AP, Focus): for 1 minute, +2 dodge DB and +2 resistance to all saves, +3 each for a Disciple or Mystic. Refocus restores it. |
| **Graceful Wind** | Press it (Engage, 1 AP, once a day): for 1 minute, +30 ft status to land Speed and +2 resistance to Reflex saves. |
| **Karmic Resilience** | When damage would take you to 0 HP, your player is asked (a reaction, once a day; not against damage that kills outright). On a yes: 1 HP, no Dying, no critical for reaching 0; and for 1 minute +2 circumstance to dying checks, +4 for a Disciple or Mystic. |
| **Staunching I–III** | The Light Bleeding flat check becomes 14 / 13 / 12. At II, a bleed at Moderate rolls a DC 16 flat check at the end of your turn; at III, a bleed at any band does. On a success the largest bleed falls by 1. |
| **Harmony Edge** | When you score a critical, you are asked before the injury roll (a reaction, once a day). On a yes it is rolled at advantage; against a minor critical's disadvantage the two cancel (B9-3). |
| **Mindful** | Press it (Engage, 1 AP, Focus). Your Wisdom is offered on your next attack roll: tick it on the attack you make with this weapon, and it is spent. Unused, it ends at the start of your next turn. Refocus restores it. |
| **Soulful Strike I–III** | Switched on and off on the weapon (Command, 1 AP), as an energy rune: 1d6 / 2d6 / 3d6 secondary force. At II and III a critical also carries a secondary force critical at −40 / −20 (B9-2). |
| **Tranquil Quiver** | Any ranged weapon: on a melee weapon it is suppressed, with no button. Press it (Engage, 1 AP, Focus). Your next ranged attack carries +2 status. On a hit against a target farther than 30 ft, the ammunition deals 1d6 precision damage of the weapon's own type (none against precision immunity). That Strike spends it, hit or miss. Refocus restores it. |

### The specific items

**Harmonious Do-maru.** An Expert Do-maru with Potency I and Harmony, at its printed figures (ACP −1, Max Dex 7, DR 3, HP 55, 15 lb; B9-9).

**Spiritblade.** A Master Nodachi with the superior trait Balanced, Potency III, Centred II and Soulful Strike II (B9-8).
- Its die is stored bare (1d10); the printed "+2" is Master's.
- Its attack bonus is what the sheet derives.
- Its fumble DC is the Nodachi's 12, less 1 for Balanced.
- On a critical hit you are offered Fear (DC 20) on the creature struck (Command, 1 AP).

### The wondrous items

| Item | What the system does |
|---|---|
| **Enlightened Prayer Beads** | Invested: +1 status Wisdom. Its Use (free, once a day) readies advantage on your next Refocus that day, taken automatically (interpretation 4). An unused one lapses at daily preparation. |
| **Lotus Blossom** | Its Use (Interact, 2 AP; refills at daily preparation) gives temporary HP equal to your level. Frightened, and any effect from a Fear or Charm source, ends. For 1 hour, advantage on saves against fear and charm: taken where the save's traits say so, offered otherwise. A second elixir the same day, from any bottle (B9-6), rolls a serious poison critical on you first. |
| **Monastic Robe of Resilience** | Invested: DR 1 as armour (the higher of it and worn armour counts). It takes armour runes, which count only while no body armour is worn, as Travellers clothing's do. |
| **Mystic Incense Burner** | Its Use burns one Incense from your gear and lights it for 1 hour (B9-7). Every creature within 30 ft of you, you included, gains +1 resistance on Will saves against fear and charm (taken where the traits say so, offered otherwise). Each is also offered +2 status on Meditation (interpretation 3). Use it again while it burns to put it out. |
| **Restorative Mat** | +2 status is offered on Meditation while it is in use (interpretation 3). Its Use (10 minutes, once a day; not in an encounter) releases Cure or Restoration from the mat at 8 PP, none of your own. Target yourself. |
| **Sash of Serenity** | Invested: +1 dodge DB, and +2 resistance on saves against fear (taken where the traits say so, offered otherwise). Its Use (once a day) gives a minute in which advantage is offered on your saves (any save, or one kind you choose). It is spent on the one you tick. |

### Monastic Items

A character with Inner Discipline sees Meditation among Create's skills for Magical items.
- The DC is Hard (+2).
- The setup dialog asks three things the system cannot judge:
  - the item's effect is a technique or innate spell you know;
  - it is not a charged item;
  - you meet the normal magic item requirements (B9-5).
- Your Meditation ranks are Create's usual ranks.

### Aging

The world setting **"Aging (Appendix)"** is on by default (B9-10). A character with no age set is unaffected.

**What is read.** The sheet's Age and race, against the Racial Age Categories table.
- The **Age Category** is shown under Age.
- The GM also sees **Natural Lifespan**. Blank uses the race's: its stated lifespan, or the middle of its range.
- Where the table prints a race's own thresholds (Dwarf, Halfling, Human, Kornargon, Tianzhi), they stand unless the GM sets another lifespan.
- Elsewhere the thresholds are 50%, 70% and 85% of the lifespan, rounded.
- Ascendan and Elves never pass Adult. An Orc does not either until the GM gives it a lifespan.

**The effect.** One effect, "Age: Old", carries the category's modifiers. They replace the previous category's. Everything derived follows them except maximum HP and the skill budget, which keep what was gained (B9-11).

**Age Resistance** (Block 8) suppresses the Strength, Dexterity and Constitution penalties while it lasts; the bonuses remain (interpretation 8).

**The GM is told once** when a character reaches their Natural Lifespan.

## For Graham

- **Mindful prints its effect after all.** The audit's B9-4 said v1.2 printed only "Activate Engage (1 AP)". The text sits on the same line as an image in the docx, and the extract missed it: "Add your Wisdom ability score to your next attack roll with the weapon, which must be made before the beginning of your next turn. The rune then becomes expended and can be restored with Refocus." That is what is built, so B9-4 needs no answer.
- **The Harmonious Do-maru prints DB +0**, but its Potency I gives armour +1 item DB. The rune is applied as printed, so the sheet shows +1. Tell me if the DB +0 should win; then Potency I would be dropped from the item.
- **Guarded's negated damage** is the critical's own dice, not dealt at all. Interpretation 5 read it as after DR; since those dice never reach DR, half of what they rolled is healed.
- **The Spiritblade's fumble DC** is the Nodachi's 12 (B9-8), less 1 for its printed Balanced, so the sheet uses 11. Tell me if the ruling meant 12 after Balanced.
- **Mindful and Tranquil Quiver** are offers on your next attack, not bound to the weapon by id. Mindful is a tick, so you choose the attack made with this weapon. Tranquil Quiver is taken on the next ranged attack whatever the weapon.

## Found in review

An independent review of the build, before release, found these; all are fixed (the core ones in TRG Prime 3.673.0's notes):
1. **Karmic Resilience asked from the GM's client could outlast the relay** (8 seconds), so the attacker's card reported the damage unapplied while the GM's client applied it. The relay now waits for a player's answer.
2. **A late yes to Guarded or Harmony Edge** still spent the reaction and the day. It no longer does.
3. **Harmony Edge was offered on a deadly critical**, where advantage changes nothing.
4. **Guarded answered weapon criticals only**; a spell attack's critical now asks too.
5. **Tranquil Quiver could be inscribed on a melee weapon**; it now needs a ranged one.
6. **An unmeasured distance read as 0 feet** for Tranquil Quiver's rider.
7. **Removing the Incense Burning effect said the incense had burned out**; the burner's Use now puts it out, saying so.

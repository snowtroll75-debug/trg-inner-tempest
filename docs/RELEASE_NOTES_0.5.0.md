# Inner Tempest 0.5.0 — Block 4: the Ghostwalker, the Kensai and the Oathblade

23 October 2026.

Needs **TRG Prime 3.668.0**. Install that first: on an older system the module warns at start-up, and most of the new classes' rules do not work.

Built to Graham's rulings of 23 October (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_RULINGS_2026-10-23.md`): all 28 of the three classes' own class feats come in now (B4-1), Weapon Discipline is untyped (B4-2), Unseen Assault is automatic or asked (B4-3), Ember Reaver is capped at Int (B4-4), and Black Lotus holds the blow until a button lands it.

## What's new

**Three classes,** in the Inner Tempest: Classes pack beside the Disciple and the Mystic. Each has its Chapter 3 table and fields, its proficiencies, its Chapter 6 wealth and its 1st-level feat:

| Class | Wealth | Feat at 1st | Proficiencies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ghostwalker | 0 | Weapon Finesse | Light armour, bucklers; simple weapons, shortbows, the wakizashi, Monastic weapons |
| Kensai | 1 | Weapon Finesse | Light and medium armour, bucklers; simple and martial weapons |
| Oathblade | 2 | Quick Draw | All armour, no shields; simple and martial weapons, the katana and wakizashi |

Each class has a **Magical Tradition** ability: its Use records the tradition, and the magical Ghost Arts name it on their cards.

**Fifteen Ghost Arts, twelve Combat Forms and four Sworn Oaths,** in the Abilities pack. **The 28 class feats** (B4-1), in the Feats pack.

## What each does

**Ghostwalker**
- **Ghost Arts (Use):** learn your arts by level: two at 1st, one more at 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th and 20th, and one per Extra Ghost Art. Each arrives as its own ability. A Focus art has its own use and its own Refocus.
- **Unseen Assault:** +1d6 precision on a hit (2d6 at 5th, 3d6 at 9th, 4d6 at 13th, 5d6 at 17th), once per round:
  - with an agile or finesse melee weapon, a thrown weapon, or a ranged weapon in its first range increment;
  - **applied by itself** when you are hidden from or undetected by the target, or it is flat-footed from your own Feint;
  - **asked on the hit** when it is flat-footed for another reason; **never** from flanking alone (B4-3).
- **Vanishing Retreat:** offered when Unseen Assault deals its damage: your reaction, a Step of 5 feet (10 with Fleeting Shadow) that triggers no reactions.
- **The arts' Use buttons** spend the AP and the use and do what the system can:
  - **Ghost Step, Spider Path, Body of Mist, Shadow Passage** arm your next token move to your Speed (no reactions where the art says so).
  - **Misdirecting Whisper** rolls Feint or Create a Diversion with advantage, then arms a Step.
  - **Silent Needles** makes two thrown Strikes, at one creature (DR once) or two.
  - **Veiled Body** and **Empty Presence** make you Invisible. A Strike ends Veiled Body; under Empty Presence a Strike makes you Concealed to the end of the turn, then Invisible again.
  - **Shadow Double** is offered when you are attacked: Concealed until your next turn or your next Strike.
  - **Hollow Breath** ends by itself when you Strike or your token moves.
  - **Smoke Veil, Caltrop Blossom, Falling Shadow** post their rule, the DC and the dice.

**Kensai**
- **Combat Form (Use):** first it learns the forms you are owed. Then, for 1 AP, it enters one as a stance and is expended until a Refocus. The form's bonuses are applied for you; all are class bonuses.
- **Flow Strike:** offered when your melee or thrown Strike hits and a Flourish is still open: another Strike at the same target at your third-attack penalty (−5 in Gale Cutter), not counted towards your penalty.
- **Weapon Discipline (Use):** choose your group. Your class bonus − 1 to attack and damage with it, untyped (B4-2), and Finesse in one hand.
- **The forms:**

  | Form | What happens |
  |---|---|
  | Black Lotus | +Int to Feint. Its Use, in the form, once a round: a Strike that holds the blow. A card at the start of the target's turn lands it (the critical, then the damage). |
  | Crashing Tide | +1 attack and +Int damage as ticks (you moved 10 ft); a critical's card offers the push (Fort vs your Technique DC). |
  | Dread Wolf | +1 attack and +Int damage against a creature with none of its allies beside it; +1 luck DB after you drop an enemy. |
  | Echoing Crane | Class bonus − 1 to DB; +1 to your next weapon attack this round after an attack misses you. |
  | Ember Reaver | +Int fire on your second hit in a round; +1 weapon damage each round in the form, to your Int, reset on a change or at combat end (B4-4). |
  | Flickering Flame | +1 per 2 Int (minimum 1) to Reflex and Acrobatics; −5 to reaction attacks against you. |
  | Gale Cutter | Flow Strike at −5; grapples against you at disadvantage; advantage on Escape. |
  | Ghost Fox | +Int to Stealth; +Int damage against a flat-footed target. |
  | Iron Tortoise | +1 DB per 2 Int (minimum 1); Shove and Reposition against you at disadvantage. |
  | Rising Phoenix | +2 per Int to Climbing and the Jumps; +Int damage after jumping into melee, as a tick. |
  | Steel Viper | +5 × Int initiative, raised in combat when you enter and lowered when you leave; once a round, a miss is asked whether to deal half damage. |
  | Stone Serpent | +Int damage from reach; advantage on Kip Up. |

**Oathblade**
- **Challenge (Use on the target):** your class bonus to melee attack and damage against it, AR equal to your class bonus, and −2 circumstance DB against everyone else. It ends when the target drops to 0 HP, dies or falls unconscious, when combat ends, or when you challenge another. A surrender: delete the effect.
- **Sworn Oath (Use):** choose the oath. It runs with each Challenge:
  - **Honour:** +2 DB against the target, and advantage on your first melee Strike against it each round, while none of your allies is beside it. An ally who attacks it, or steps beside it, suppresses the oath until your next turn.
  - **Guardianship:** the target takes −2 on attacks against anyone but you. The first time each round it damages an ally, your next melee Strike against it has advantage and +Cha damage (minimum 1).
  - **Vengeance:** the first time each round it damages you or an ally, Retribution: advantage, +Cha damage and AR +2 on your next melee Strike against it. It ends with that Strike (with Unpaid Debt, when you damage it).
  - **Fealty:** at daily preparation you are asked for your Sworn Charge. During a Challenge the charge has +1 and AR 1 against the target, and each of you gives the other +2 on the next attack against it after damaging it.
- **Unwavering Purpose, Considered Mind:** Charisma (or Intelligence) on Refocus when higher. **Oathblade's Resolve:** +1 conviction.

**The feats with a button or an offer:** Blended Form, Fluid Blending, Echoed Form, Form Reversal, Formless Cut, Perfect Tempo, Ghost Art Mastery, Shadow Counter, Shadowy Step, Claim the Next Foe, Formal Declaration, Shared Purpose, Deflect Arrow. **The feats that simply apply:** Ambusher's Patience, Ghost Blade, Disciplined Follow-Through, Distant Honour, Guarded Commitment, Unpaid Debt, Extra Form, Extra Ghost Art, Fleeting Shadow, Shelter Under Oath.

## Not automated, said on the items

- **Ghostwalk, Between Glances, Walk the Empty Space, No Trace, Smoke Eyes, Solitary Supremacy, Dancing Leaf:** observation, tracking, Aid and positioning are the GM's.
- **Smoke Veil and Caltrop Blossom** don't place their areas; the cards give the DC, the dice and the duration.
- **Oath of Honour:** "your challenged target cannot gain the benefits of flanking you".
- **Oath of Fealty and Shelter Under Oath:** "within 30 feet" and "conscious" are the GM's.
- **Weapon Discipline's free hand** for Disarm and Shove: the system does not check free hands, so nothing is needed.
- **Black Lotus's landed blow** carries the damage and the critical, not the riders, precision dice or declared AR the Strike would have added.

## For Graham

1. **Deflect Arrow needs Evade, and Evade needs Inner Discipline.** The feat's trigger is "take the Evade Action". A Kensai or Oathblade without Inner Discipline can take the feat and never use it. It is offered only to a character who can Evade. Should Deflect Arrow work without Evade for these classes (for example, as its own reaction against a ranged attack)?
2. **The wakizashi** is in no pack yet (Chapter 6's weapons come later). The Ghostwalker and the Oathblade are registered as proficient with it by name, ready for it.
3. **Iron Tortoise and the Trip.** "All attempts to push or move you" is read as Shove and Reposition. A Trip knocks you prone without moving you, so it is left out.
4. **Form Reversal** is a Flourish reaction on another creature's turn. It is not counted against your turn's Flourish, and is offered even after you used one. Should it be?

## Found before the live test

An independent review of the new code, before anything ran in Foundry, found nine defects. All are fixed and checked:
- **Land the blow:** the GM posted the card, so a player could not mark it used and the press failed. The card is now posted on the attacker's player's screen.
- **A blow held when combat ended** blocked Black Lotus for good. It is now cleared at combat end, and an old one never blocks a new Strike.
- **Changing Challenge** could make two screens delete the same oath effects, and abort. Each delete now checks the effect is still there.
- **Ember Reaver** counted a Flow Strike's hit before the hit that led to it, so the second hit got no fire.
- **Steel Viper's initiative** could drift when two screens adjusted it. It now moves by the difference from what the combatant already carries.
- **Deflect Arrow** gave +4 even when Evade was refused.
- **Ghost Art Mastery's** second use arrived only at the next Refocus. It now arrives at once.
- **Dread Wolf and Claim the Next Foe** fired on more damage to a creature already down. Now: the blow that drops it; Dread Wolf on an enemy; Claim the Next Foe on your own blow ("you disable a foe").
- **The GM's screen** read only the scene it was looking at. The combat's scene is read too, and Oath of Honour is re-armed only when the board can say no ally is beside the target.

# Inner Tempest 0.6.0: Block 5, the racial feats

24 October 2026.

Needs **TRG Prime 3.669.0**. Install that first. On an older system the module warns at start-up, and most of the new feats' Uses and offers do not work.

Built to Graham's rulings of 24 October (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_RULINGS_2026-10-24.md`) and the Block 5 audit (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK5_AUDIT.md`):
1. **B5-1** Water Bolt comes into Block 5, in the Spells pack.
2. **B5-2** The Elemental spell lists go by trait, with four additions.
3. **B5-3** Adaptive Mind is conviction's Advantage on a Mental Focus check, refunded on a Success or better.
4. **B5-4** Foxfire's motes are placed and moved on the map. They are **light circles, not tokens**; see "For Graham" below.

**The core racial feats opened to the four races** (the book's "Existing racial feats", FR-48): 33 entries, 25 feats. A bracketed heritage limits a feat to that heritage: Bestial Resilience (Mujina) is offered only to a Mujina Miraku. Interpretation 1 reads a core heritage in a core prerequisite as the bracketed one (Fire-Like Blood's "Azgar or Elarni heritage" is the Zenki's or the Kasai's).

**Water Bolt** (PP 2, arcane and verdant, 4d4 bludgeoning), in the Spells pack. Its 5-foot push on a serious or deadly critical injury is named on the card and moved by hand.

**Draconic Armament's Bite and Claw** in the Natural Attacks pack.

## How some of them work

- **The save techniques** (Blinding Majesty, Celestial Breath, Valley Chill, Web Snare, the two venoms) roll each creature's save against your Level DC through the system's area rail (3.669.0), and apply the ladder's conditions. Blinding Majesty leaves your allies out. Speed penalties, Valley Chill's −1 Reflex and Web Snare's hold are this module's effects on the creature, laid through the GM when it is not yours.
- **Durations** follow the system's round reading: "until the beginning of its (or your) next turn" is a round, "until the end of its next turn" two (audit interpretation 3, revised).
- **Web Snare's** Immobilisation carries its Escape DC (your Level DC); a successful Escape removes it. A new Immobilisation frees the last creature.
- **Spirit Roar** rolls one Demoralise and meets it against each enemy within 15 feet at that enemy's Level DC, as Demoralise against it. Each creature affected is immune to your Spirit Roar until the combat ends.
- **The Elemental spell choices** list the printed spells carrying a trait of your heritage's element, with Graham's additions: Magic Stone for the Tsuchi; Water Breathing and Water Walk for the Suiryoku; Endure Elements for every element. Each arrives as an innate spell with the Focus trait, cast with Wisdom.
- **Spirit Beacon** lights your token (bright 20 feet, dim 20 feet further) and raises the light level (three steps within 20 feet, one beyond). You and your allies in the bright light carry its two ticks while it lasts.
- **Mist Veil** conceals every creature within 10 feet of you, laid and lifted as they come and go, until the start of your next turn. That those outside are concealed to those within is the table's.
- **Living Lantern** (Block 1) now lights the token dim to 10 feet and raises the light level a step, instead of asking the table to set the light.
- **Raptorial Refinement** changes the Raptorial Blade itself (AR 2, Versatile P); removing the feat puts it back.
- **Wings Unfurled** gives a Fly Speed equal to your land Speed, and Wing Burst's card says you may end in the air.
- **Elemental Attunement's** +1 reaches only your innate spells of your element: their attacks and their save DCs (3.669.0 tells an innate spell from a prepared one).
- **Guardian's Intercession** is not offered when its Focus use is spent or you cannot move to the ally (immobilised, grabbed, paralysed, petrified, unconscious, dying).
- **Fortune's Reversal** on an attack: when your attack could fumble, its fumble check is made as soon as you roll, and if the fumble stands you are offered the reroll before anything else happens. Only the attack roll is made again.

## For Graham

1. **Foxfire's motes are light circles, not tokens (B5-4).** A token on the map is read as a creature by flanking, reactive strikes, area saves and the reaction checks, so a mote would flank, catch a Fireball and, as a token of its caster, offer the caster's reactions. Each mote is instead a 10-foot circle (as Inner Sanctum's is) that raises the light level a step where it stands. You place them with the area aim, up to three, within 60 feet. Use Foxfire again to move them: once a turn, up to 30 feet each, still within 60 feet. They go after 1 minute. Is that acceptable, or would you rather the card state the rule and the GM place lights?
2. **Spirit Beacon's light levels.** "Bright light" is read as three steps (the core's Luminance prints "A bright light … increasing the light level by three steps") and dim light as one (Ember Of Devotion's "dim light (+1 light level)"). Confirm?
3. **Guardian's Intercession's Step.** The card says to Step beside the ally if you must; the system does not move your token. Is that acceptable, or should the Use ask where to Step?

## Found in review

An independent review before release found these; all are fixed.
1. Spirit Roar spent its AP and Focus use before the first Demoralise was rolled, so cancelling the dialog cost them. It is paid once the first roll is made.
2. Waiting Blade was not offered when a reaction's melee Strike missed you. The book excludes none.
3. Web Snare cast again on the creature it already held left the old web on. The new web replaces it.
4. Foxfire's circles were looked for on the scene in view, not the one they were placed on.
5. Elemental Attunement reached prepared spells (it is innate spells only now; see above).
6. Guardian's Intercession was offered when expended or held fast.

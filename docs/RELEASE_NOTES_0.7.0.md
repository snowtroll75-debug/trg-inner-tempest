# Inner Tempest 0.7.0: Block 6, the remaining class feats

24 October 2026.

Needs **TRG Prime 3.670.0**. Install that first. On an older system the module warns at start-up, and most of the new feats' declarations are not read.

Built to Graham's rulings of 24 October (B6-1 to B6-10, `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_RULINGS_2026-10-24.md`; all ten proposals adopted) and the Block 6 audit (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK6_AUDIT.md`).

## What is new

**The fourteen class feats of Chapter 5 not built before**, in the Feats pack, each printing the book's text (read from the docx) followed by what the system does with it. Drunken Stance's own action, **Drunken Strike**, is in the Abilities pack and is added when you take the stance.

| How | Feats |
|---|---|
| **A stance** (Use, 1 AP, the system's stance rules: one at a time, Stance Mastery's second, Reactive Stance's free entry) | Crane, Dragon, Drunken, Mantis, Monkey, Snake, Tiger, Wolf |
| **A Use** | Drunken Strike, Perfect Shot, Perfect Strike, Inner Magic, Mystic Essence |
| **Read by another ability** | Elemental Fist (Inner Strike), Stunning Fist (Flurry of Blows) |

**The core class feats opened to the five classes** (the book's "Existing class feats", FC-55): 33 entries, 21 feats, registered through the system's class access. The Mystic's "Metamagic Feats where compatible with innate spells" names no feat: the Metamagic feats a Mystic may use are Adept feats, open to it already, and now work on its innate spells (below).

## How they work

**The stances.** Each one's benefits ride on its effect, so they last exactly as long as the stance:

| Stance | While in it |
|---|---|
| Crane (Devoted or Primal Path) | +2 circumstance DB; +5 on High Jump and Long Jump, applied for you (the book's "reduce the difficulty by 5") |
| Dragon (Primal or Transcendent Path) | +2 circumstance AB; Sweep on your fists, gauntlets and Monastic weapons (B6-2); on each Stride, the first square of difficult terrain costs nothing extra |
| Drunken (Devoted or Primal Path) | +2 circumstance DB; Drunken Strike may be used |
| Mantis | Deadly on your unarmed attacks; +2 circumstance Reflex; the first Move action of each of your turns triggers no reactions (B6-4) |
| Monkey | +2 circumstance Acrobatics and Climbing, and +2 on High Jump and Long Jump; a 1-AP jump carries no +2 to its DC (B6-5) |
| Snake (Inner Discipline; Devoted or Illuminator Path) | When a creature misses you with a melee Strike, you are asked whether to strike back with your reaction (a melee Strike, any weapon in hand or your fists, no multiple attack penalty). It is an Attack reaction, so Battle Readiness's may take it (B6-9) |
| Tiger | Your unarmed attacks deal slashing, without asking (B6-1); bleeding you inflict is 1 higher; your Step is 10 feet while your Speed is at least 20 |
| Wolf | Your unarmed attacks deal piercing, without asking (B6-1); Pack Attack (+1d6 precision when two allies threaten the target) |

A Path is not a feat, so the feat picker cannot check "Devoted or Primal Monastic Path"; the stance's Use refuses when your Path is not one it names.

**Drunken Strike** (Attack, Flourish, 1 AP; only in Drunken Stance): a melee Strike at −2 circumstance. If it deals damage, its total stands as your Feint check against the target's own Feint DC, and Feint's result applies: a Success leaves the target flat-footed (B6-3). Nothing is rolled again.

**Perfect Shot and Perfect Strike** (Attack, Flourish, 2 AP): a ranged Strike (a bow, a crossbow, a javelin) or a melee Strike with advantage, counted as two attacks for your multiple attack penalty. The Zen Archer trait is kept for Block 7 (B6-10).

**Inner Magic** chooses one cantrip of your Path's tradition; **Mystic Essence** one spell whose base PP is no greater than your level. Each arrives as an innate spell cast with your Path's ability, once; delete it to choose again. Inner Magic also opens the feats with the Mystic trait (B6-7).

**Elemental Fist** adds electricity (Air), bludgeoning (Earth), fire (Fire) and cold (Water) to Inner Strike's choices and to its card's type buttons. The element's trait is on your attacks while that Inner Strike lasts, and changes with the type.

**Stunning Fist.** After Flurry of Blows, when both Strikes were made and either of the two hit and dealt damage (Disciplined Flurry's third is not one of them), the creature saves Fortitude against your Technique DC (10 + your Path's ability + your level, B6-6): stunned 1 on a Partial or Failure, stunned 3 on a Fumble or Blunder. A Partial is not a success in TRG Prime, and the book says "must succeed".

**The Mystic's Metamagic (B6-8).** A Mystic's Path-stamped innate spells (Mystical Power's, Mystic Essence's, a discipline's), cantrips excepted, offer its Metamagic feats when cast. Their cost is paid from the spell's free warping budget (your level), as its warps are, priced on the spell's printed PP; nothing comes from your pool. Economic Spell is not offered there, as there is no pool cost to lower. Spells a Mystic already has are marked when the GM loads the world.

**What a miss is.** Snake Stance answers a Strike that was resolved and missed (TRG Prime 3.670.0 says so with the Strike). An attack never resolved, or a hit that lands nothing (a grab), is not a miss. Waiting Blade, Steel Viper, Echoing Crane, Form Reversal and Shadow Counter read the same.

**One Move per turn (Mantis Stance).** Each drag of the token is read as one move, so a Stride split into two drags spends the free move on the first. Make the free Stride in one drag.

## Interpretations

These stand unless Graham says otherwise (audit, "Interpretations"):
1. Circumstance bonuses do not stack: with Stance Mastery holding Crane and Monkey, only the +5 applies to a jump.
2. Mystic Essence is taken once.
3. Perfect Shot's and Strike's advantage is on that Strike only.
4. Tiger Stance's 20-foot Speed is your land Speed at the moment, penalties included.
5. Dragon Stance's first square is per Stride.

## Found in review

An independent review before release found these; all are fixed.
1. **Inner Magic opened the Mystic's whole list of core feats** (Accurate Spell, Combat Reflexes, Injury Relief) to a Disciple. It now opens only feats carrying the Mystic trait, as B6-7 rules (TRG Prime 3.670.0 keeps the two apart).
2. **The Mystic's Metamagic feats were charged to the PP pool** on an innate spell, and priced as if the spell cost 0 (Temporal Extension was free). They are spent from the free budget and priced on the printed PP (core).
3. **A cantrip a discipline grants was marked for Metamagic.** Cantrips are never marked, and the system refuses Metamagic on an innate cantrip however it is marked (core).
4. **Snake Stance offered only the fists and Monastic weapons.** The book says "a melee strike"; any melee weapon in hand is offered.
5. **Perfect Shot refused a javelin**, and the melee Strikes (Perfect Strike, Drunken Strike, Snake Stance, Predator's Pounce, Formless Cut) could pick one and have it thrown. TRG Prime's ranged modes are missile and thrown; both are now read.
6. **Stunning Fist counted Disciplined Flurry's third Strike.** "If either Strike hits" is the Flurry's two.
7. **Snake Stance (and the other miss reactions) could answer an attack that was never resolved, or a grab.** They now answer a miss as the system announces it.
8. Core, in TRG Prime 3.670.0: Drunken Strike's Feint no longer takes a waiting Inspire Competence (or Cutting Words, Counter Perform, Seer's Vision, a rod); Tiger Stance's bleed is no longer added to an injury you deal yourself (a poison's critical); and Mantis Stance's first move is recorded only while you hold the stance, so a move made before entering it does not spend it.

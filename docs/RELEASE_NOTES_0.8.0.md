# Inner Tempest 0.8.0: Block 7, the archetypes

25 October 2026.

Needs **TRG Prime 3.671.0**. Install that first. On an older system the module warns at start-up, and most of the archetypes' declarations are not read.

Built to Graham's rulings of 25 October (B7-1 to B7-8, `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_RULINGS_2026-10-25.md`; all eight proposals adopted) and the Block 7 audit (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK7_AUDIT.md`).

## What is new

**Twenty of Chapter 5's twenty-one archetypes**, in the Feats pack with the category Archetype, so the sheet's archetype picker offers them by their printed class traits and prerequisites and checks their progression line. Each prints the book's text (read from the docx) followed by what the system does with it. **Dragon Tamer** comes with the Dragon Companions in Block 10 (B7-1).

**The seven abilities they print as items**, in the Abilities pack, added when you take the archetype: Twin Dragon, Drawn Verdict, Restorative Palm, Night Lotus Venom, Bound Challenge, Wandering Creed and Turning the Blade. Powerful Fists (Brawler) and the Soul-Bound Weapon are worked into their archetypes.

**The core archetypes opened to the five classes** (the book's "Existing feats", FA-22): Assassin and Mastermind for the Disciple and the Ghostwalker; Mastermind and Vanguard for the Kensai; Mastermind and Runemage for the Mystic; Archer, Armour Master, Cavalier and Champion for the Oathblade. The system's own tier rule still stands beside them (B7-8).

## How they work

| Archetype | What the system does |
|---|---|
| **Brawler** | Your fists deal 1d6 with Versatile B, +1 to the total at BAB 3 and every 3 after, magical at BAB 3, cold iron and silver at 8, adamantine at 16, as the Disciple's Powerful Fists. Class feats with the Disciple trait are offered to you (B7-3). Progression: character level 10 (B7-2). |
| **Formbinder** | While you hold a form, Combat Form's Use asks whether to add the new one beside it or replace it. Two forms make one stance named "Formbinder: A + B"; a Blended Form fills both places (B7-7). One form is activated a turn. Where both forms improve the same thing, only the greater applies. You gain **Twin Dragon**, a form outside the Kensai's table: the first time each round your Strike hits, +1 class DB until your next turn; the first time each round an attack misses you, +1 damage until the end of your next turn. |
| **Harmony Warden** | +4 class to Refocus, Healing Trance and Power Recovery, offered as a tick-box ("in a wilderness environment", the GM's call). Use once a day for the trance: a Verdant spell of the Utility category, base PP up to 2 (4 at 5th level, 6 at 10th…, B7-4), cast once as an innate spell. |
| **Iaijutsu Master** | +25 class to Initiative with a sword sheathed: a Sword-group weapon you carry and are not wielding (B7-5). **Drawn Verdict:** with your Challenge on a target in reach and a sword sheathed, 1 AP draws it and Strikes; the target is offered no reaction to it, and if it deals damage the target cannot react until its next turn. At the end of your turn you are asked whether to sheathe it again. |
| **Inner Adept** | You gain Inner Discipline. Use chooses your class skill (Meditation or Mental Focus) and a Monastic Path, which must match your class's tradition where it has one. Class feats with the Disciple trait are offered (B7-3). Progression reads the class skill you chose. |
| **Jade Physician** | Medicine is a class skill; +2 class to Treat Injury, Treat Toxin and Treat Wounds. **Restorative Palm** (2 AP, touch, a living creature other than you): 4 HP per level, or Bleeding reduced by 1 per two levels, or a new save against one of its poisons or diseases. The target is then immune to it for an hour. |
| **Lotus Enchanter / Onmyoji Necromancer** | Mystic Essence's list also offers specialist spells with the Enchantment (Soul) trait, within its PP limit; that spell carries +1 class to its attack checks and save DC. |
| **Night Lotus** | Medicine is a class skill; you resist poison. **Night Lotus Venom** (1 AP) coats a piercing or slashing weapon, or ammunition, until the end of your next turn or the first creature it damages. That creature saves Fortitude against your Level DC: Critical unaffected; Success or Partial −5 feet to Speed; Failure Slowed 1; Fumble Paralyzed, then Slowed 1. After Unseen Assault the save is at −1 (the DC is raised by 1). |
| **Oathbound** | Mental Focus is a class skill; the katana and wakizashi are martial weapons for you. **Bound Challenge**: the Oathblade's Challenge, paid with a conviction point. |
| **Ronin** | Deception is a class skill. **Wandering Creed** asks at each daily preparation which Sworn Oath to hold. **Turning the Blade** is offered as your reaction when a creature other than your challenged target damages you with a Strike: your Challenge moves to it, free. |
| **Shadowdancer** | +2 class to Stealth; you gain Stealthy Strike and Shadowy Step. |
| **Shugenja** | Learned casters only (the Cleric, Druid and Wizard; IT-C9). Use chooses your element; at each daily preparation choose two spells, any tradition, with one of its traits (not specialist spells), granted as Focus innate spells. |
| **Soul Weapon Master** | At daily preparation (or Use) bond a melee weapon: +1 to its damage at BAB 3 and every 3 after, magical at BAB 3, cold iron and silver at 8, adamantine at 16; Flurry of Blows and the other unarmed abilities may be used with it (B7-6). |
| **Steel Disciple** | Use chooses your class skill and one Kensai Combat Form, entered as a stance with no Combat Form to spend. Class feats with the Kensai trait are offered (B7-3). |
| **Tanuki Trickster** | +2 class to Deception; Shape Of The Humanoid and Mirage Entity as Focus innate spells, cast with your Path's tradition, or your class's chosen Magical Tradition without one (IT-M6). |
| **Twin Path** | Use chooses a second Mystical Discipline: its ability, and its 1st-level spells as Focus innate spells. |
| **Veilborn** | Use learns one more Focus Ghost Art (not counted against the table). +2 class to Refocus restoring a Ghost Art. After Shadow Double, Veiled Body, Empty Presence or Shadow Passage you gain +1 class DB until your next turn; after Smoke Veil you are asked whether you are inside the smoke first. |
| **Way of the Geisha** | You gain Affable; +2 class to Artistry, Diplomacy and Perform. |
| **Zen Archer** | Proficient with the longbow, short bow and composite bows; Perfect Shot granted; Flurry of Blows and the other unarmed abilities work with a bow, not a crossbow (B7-6: the abilities, not the fists' damage). You count as having the Archer archetype for its class feats. |

**A Strike its target cannot react to** (Drawn Verdict) offers none of this module's reactions either: Form Reversal, Shadow Counter, Waiting Blade and Snake Stance stay silent.

**Tradition for a class without a Path (IT-M6).** A Ghostwalker, Kensai or Oathblade's chosen Magical Tradition now stamps the innate spells waiting for one, as soon as it is chosen.

## Fixed in Block 6

**Mystic Essence and Mystical Power offered specialist spells.** A specialist spell belongs to a class's own list, not the tradition's; they are no longer offered, except the ones Lotus Enchanter and Onmyoji Necromancer open.

## Interpretations

These stand unless Graham says otherwise (audit, "Interpretations"):
1. Shugenja's spells are any tradition, with either of the element's traits, never specialist spells; they refresh at each daily preparation.
2. Restorative Palm's new save is the affliction's own stage save, now. Its hour of immunity covers all three uses.
3. Night Lotus Venom's Partial is the Success row (IT-m24).
4. Inner Adept's Path matches the class's tradition where the class has one.
5. Iaijutsu Master's +25 is a class bonus and stacks with other class bonuses to Initiative.
6. Harmony Warden's +4 is a tick-box; it stacks with Veilborn's +2 (IT-M24).
7. Veilborn's +1 DB follows Shadow Double, Veiled Body, Empty Presence, Shadow Passage and Smoke Veil.
8. Oathbound's katana and wakizashi are martial for the holder, so a Martial class is proficient with them.
9. The class-trait lines are any-of, as the system reads core archetypes.

## Found in review

An independent review before release found these; all are fixed.
1. **Drawn Verdict's reach and Restorative Palm's touch never refused anyone:** the distance was read without waiting for it. A creature too far is now refused.
2. **Restorative Palm on another player's character** failed with a permission error after its AP and use were spent (the Bleeding, the new save and the hour's immunity are writes to the target). They are now made by the target's owner, or by the GM, who posts the card.
3. **Steel Disciple's Kensai feats, and Dancing Leaf, Deflect Arrow and Shadowy Step for a Brawler or Inner Adept, were never offered:** the picker refused them on their class tag before reading the opened trait (core, TRG Prime 3.671.0).
4. **Veilborn's +2 to Refocus applied only on a Shift-click.** The check dialog was not told which ability the Refocus restores; it now asks for the ability first and offers the +2 (core).
5. **Wandering Creed and Sworn Oath:** the oath Wandering Creed gives now counts as Sworn Oath's choice, so Sworn Oath cannot add a second oath beside it. A Ronin holding the Oath of Fealty is asked for the Sworn Charge after choosing the creed, not by a second question at the same moment that could write to the oath being replaced.
6. **A lost Soul-Bound Weapon could be replaced the next day** without a daily preparation. It now waits for the daily preparation.
7. **Night Lotus Venom was delivered by any Strike.** You now choose the weapon (or ammunition) you coat, and only its Strikes (a missile Strike, for ammunition) deliver it.
8. **Drawn Verdict spent its AP and use when the attack was cancelled.** A cancelled Strike now puts the sword back and costs nothing. **Harmony Warden's card** now follows a cast made, not one refused.
9. Core: an Enigma Wizard (an instinctive caster) is no longer offered Shugenja.

**Left as they are:** an Enchantment or Soul spell chosen with Mystic Essence *before* taking Lotus Enchanter or Onmyoji Necromancer does not gain the +1 (delete and choose it again). Drawn Verdict and Restorative Palm check distance only when both creatures have tokens on the scene.

# Inner Tempest rulings, 25 October 2026 (Block 7: archetypes)

Graham adopted every proposal in `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK7_AUDIT.md` ("Adopt all proposals").

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| B7-1 | Dragon Tamer gives a Dragon Companion (Block 10). | **Built with the Dragon Companions in Block 10**, egg prerequisite and all. Block 7 ships the other 20 archetypes. |
| B7-2 | Brawler's progression "Level +10". | **Character level 10**, as the other twenty lines read "+10" as a total. |
| B7-3 | "Feats which are available only to the Disciple (Martial) class" (Brawler, Inner Adept); "feats which have the Kensai (Martial) trait" (Steel Disciple). | **Class feats with the Disciple / Kensai trait** (as B6-7). Not the core feats the book opens to the class, and not every Martial feat. |
| B7-4 | Harmony Warden's daily innate spell. | **A Verdant spell of the Utility category, chosen when the trance is entered, base PP up to 2 (4 at 5th level, 6 at 10th, and so on)**, under the usual innate limit. |
| B7-5 | Iaijutsu Master's "a sword sheathed". | **A weapon of the Sword group that you carry and are not wielding** (on you, not packed away). Drawn Verdict draws it as part of its 1 AP. |
| B7-6 | Soul-Bound Weapon and Zen Archer: "any ability that may be used with unarmed strikes … may also be used with" it. | **Abilities only** (Flurry of Blows, Stunning Fist, Inner Strike, Elemental Fist and the like), not the fists' damage die, scaling or materials. An ability that needs a melee attack still needs one. |
| B7-7 | Formbinder with Blended Form. | **A blend fills both of Formbinder's places.** |
| B7-8 | The system's tier rule for core archetypes. | **Kept.** The book's lists add to it. |

The audit's nine interpretations stand as written.

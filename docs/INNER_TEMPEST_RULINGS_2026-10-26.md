# Inner Tempest rulings, 26 October 2026 (Block 8: the Grimoire)

Graham adopted every proposal in `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK8_AUDIT.md`: "Adopt all" for B8-1 to B8-8, and "Three small ones now" for B8-9.

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| B8-1 | Ability Inversion: which numbers swap? | **The totals.** The highest takes the lowest's total and the reverse; later bonuses add on top. Ties are the caster's choice. A Partial is the Success row (IT-m24); a Blunder is the Fumble row. |
| B8-2 | Acidic Touch: which hits call for the save? | **Any melee Strike that hits**, at your spell save DC, once per hit. Sickened stays until removed. |
| B8-3 | Arrows Flight: "no bigger than a normal arrow". | **Arrows, bolts, sling bullets, darts and shuriken**, up to 30 of one stack split off and marked. A javelin or thrown spear needs the +4 PP warp. |
| B8-4 | Disrupt Soul and Spiritual Distraction: whose DC, and who rolls? | **The DC of the casting that laid the spell**, recorded on the target. The system rolls the save at each casting (Distraction before the spell check; Disrupt Soul once the casting is paid for). "The spell ends" ends Distraction or Disrupt Soul itself. |
| B8-5 | Disrupt Soul: "1d8 per PP spent". | **The PP actually spent.** An innate spell or magical Focus ability uses the per-four-levels line. |
| B8-6 | Foretell Failure with a DC the GM sets afterwards. | **The card asks the GM**, and the check waits. A known DC is answered by the system. |
| B8-7 | Tranquil Mastery: when is the second ability chosen? | **With the first, before the roll.** |
| B8-8 | Age Resistance: aging comes with Block 9. | **The spell ships now**, marking the creature; Block 9's aging rules read the mark. |
| B8-9 | Block 3's partly automated spells. | **Three now:** Burst of Speed's second round, Timeless Motion's movement that provokes nothing, Water Bolt's push. **Dragon Fear, Aura of Heat and Burning Stride** wait for their own core block after Block 10. |

The audit's six interpretations stand as written.

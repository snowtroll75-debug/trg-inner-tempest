# Inner Tempest rulings, 24 October 2026 (Block 5)

Graham's answers to the Block 5 audit (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK5_AUDIT.md`). All four proposals were taken.

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| B5-1 | Elemental Power lists "Water Bolt (new spell)", which is in the Grimoire (Block 8). | **Bring Water Bolt into Block 5,** in the Spells pack. |
| B5-2 | Matching the Tianzhi spell lists to an element by trait leaves Water Walk, Water Breathing and Endure Elements with no element, and Magic Stone with Hikari, so a Tsuchi has nothing for Elemental Power. | **By trait, plus the fixes:** Water Walk and Water Breathing for Suiryoku, Magic Stone for Tsuchi, and Endure Elements for every element. |
| B5-3 | Adaptive Mind: "When you use a conviction point to reroll a Mental Focus skill check". The core's conviction has no reroll. | **Conviction's Advantage on a Mental Focus check, refunded** when the result is a success or better. |
| B5-4 | Foxfire's three movable motes of light: a player cannot place lights on the scene. | **Light tokens:** the Use creates three small light tokens the player can move, removed after 1 minute. |

The eight interpretations in the audit stand unless Graham says otherwise.

## Block 6 (24 October, 19:03): the class feats

Graham's answer to the ten questions of `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK6_AUDIT.md`: **the proposals, all ten.** The audit's interpretations stand.

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| B6-1 | Tiger and Wolf Stance: "your attacks" deal slashing or piercing. | **Your unarmed attacks** (fists, gauntlets, Monastic weapons) deal that type while in the stance, without asking. A weapon keeps its own. |
| B6-2 | Dragon Stance's Sweep. | **Your unarmed attacks.** |
| B6-3 | Drunken Strike: "your total Attack Roll as the Feint DC". | **Your attack total stands as your Feint check**, against the target's usual Feint DC (Hard). Feint's own degrees apply. |
| B6-4 | Mantis Stance: "one Move action each turn that does not provoke reactions". | **The first Move action each turn** in the stance, automatically. |
| B6-5 | Monkey Stance: "no penalty for using these actions for 1 AP". | **The 1-AP High Jump and Long Jump carry no +2 to their DC.** |
| B6-6 | Stunning Fist's "Technique DC". | **10 + your Monastic Path's ability + your level.** A Blunder stuns 3, as a Fumble. |
| B6-7 | Inner Magic: feats "available only to the Mystic (Adept) class". | **Feats with the Mystic trait**, not every Adept feat. |
| B6-8 | The Mystic's "Metamagic Feats where compatible with innate spells". | **Opened to the Mystic, and usable on its innate spells** (not cantrips), paid from the spell's PP. A feat that needs a spellbook or preparation is not compatible. |
| B6-9 | Snake Stance's reaction has no traits. | **It is a Strike, so it has the Attack trait**; Battle Readiness's reaction may take it. |
| B6-10 | Perfect Shot names the Zen Archer (Block 7). | **The trait is kept**; the feat opens to a Zen Archer when Block 7 registers the archetype. |

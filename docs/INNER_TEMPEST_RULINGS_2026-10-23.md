# Inner Tempest rulings, 23 October 2026 (Block 4)

Graham's answers to the Block 4 audit (`docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK4_AUDIT.md`). All four proposals were taken, and a fifth question, on Black Lotus, was answered in chat.

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| B4-1 | A 1st-level Ghostwalker, Kensai or Oathblade must take a class feat, and their own class feats were planned for Block 6. | **Bring all 28 into Block 4.** These are the three classes' own class feats: 11 Ghostwalker, 9 Kensai and 9 Oathblade, with Deflect Arrow shared. The Disciple and Mystic stances, and the core class feats opened to the new classes (FC-55), stay in Block 6. |
| B4-2 | Weapon Discipline's "class bonus − 1" and the Combat Forms' class bonuses don't stack under the typed rules. | **Weapon Discipline's bonus is untyped.** The forms' class bonuses add on top of it. |
| B4-3 | Unseen Assault needs the target flat-footed "as a direct result of your own Stealth, Deception or Ghostwalker abilities". | **Automatic, otherwise a tick-box.** It applies when you are hidden from or undetected by the target, or the target is flat-footed from your own Feint or Ghost Art. When the target is flat-footed for another reason, it is asked as a tick-box on the attack. Flanking alone never counts. |
| BL | Black Lotus: "delay your damage and critical rolls until the start of the enemy's turn". Roll on the hit and apply later, or hold the roll? | **Held, landed by button.** Nothing is rolled on the hit. At the start of the target's turn a card offers Land the blow, which rolls the critical and the damage. |
| B4-4 | Ember Reaver's "+1 to weapon damage each consecutive round in this form" has no cap. | **Capped at your Int modifier.** It resets when you change form or combat ends. |

The twelve interpretations in the audit stand unless Graham says otherwise.

## After 0.5.0: the four questions in its release notes

Answered by Graham on 23 October 2026, after the 0.5.0 live test. Built in 0.5.1 (on TRG Prime 3.668.1).

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Deflect Arrow's trigger is "take the Evade Action", and Evade needs Inner Discipline, so a Kensai or Oathblade without it can take the feat and never use it. Should it work without Evade? | **Keep needing Evade.** No change. |
| Q2 | The wakizashi is in no pack yet. | **Add the weapons from Chapter 6** (weapons only, not the armour or other gear). All sixteen are in the new Inner Tempest: Weapons pack, with the Backswing, Brace and Twin traits built in TRG Prime 3.668.1. |
| Q3 | Iron Tortoise: "All attempts to push or move you are made at disadvantage." Was read as Shove and Reposition, leaving out a Trip. | **A Trip is included.** Shove, Reposition and Trip against you are at disadvantage. |
| Q4 | Form Reversal is a Flourish reaction on another creature's turn. Should it count against your turn's Flourish? | **No.** No change. |

## After 0.5.1: the three questions in its release notes

Answered by Graham on 23 October 2026, after the 0.5.1 live test. All three keep 0.5.1 as built.

| # | Question | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Q5 | The Butterfly Sword's 20 gp: one sword, or the pair? | **20 gp is per sword.** The item stays one sword; Double Slice and Twin need two. |
| Q6 | Should Backswing take back a Black Lotus held blow that lands for no damage? | **No.** A held blow counts when it is made. |
| Q7 | Twin: any two Twin weapons, or a matched pair? | **Any two Twin weapons count.** |

Graham also asked for the two Double Slice problems the 0.5.1 test found to be fixed (both swings' criticals were inflicted; the card asked for damage rolls already made). Done in TRG Prime 3.668.2.

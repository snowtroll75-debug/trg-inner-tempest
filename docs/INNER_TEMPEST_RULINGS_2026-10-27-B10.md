# Inner Tempest rulings, 27 October 2026: Block 10 (Dragon Companions)

Graham, on `docs/INNER_TEMPEST_BLOCK10_AUDIT.md`: "Go with your recommendations". Every proposal is adopted.

| # | Ruling |
|---|---|
| B10-1 | **Level:** base level plus the master's levels gained since hatching, as printed. The whole table is built, so a dragon the GM raises still grows correctly. |
| B10-2 | **Size and Str/Dex:** the table alone. A size step does not add the core's own Str/Dex change on top (core C6), and the printed base blocks are final at their base size. |
| B10-3 | **Sweep:** the Tail Slap gains the Sweep trait at Base +20, as the table lists it. |
| B10-4 | **The earth dragon** has wings but does not fly: the Wings attack, Wing Buffet and Deflecting Wings as for the others, and no fly speed. |
| B10-5 | **Wing Buffet** uses the core Wings damage at the dragon's size, whether or not the Wings attack has been granted yet. |
| B10-6 | **Spells known:** +1 for each level gained, with the maximum PP the system's for its caster level (level − 3). |
| B10-7 | **The fire and light dragons' skill ranks** ship as printed, and the sheet shows the overspend. |
| B10-8 | **Skill points:** 8 + Intelligence. |
| B10-9 | **The light dragon's fourth feat** is the GM's extra: granted free, and the budget stays at three. |
| B10-10 | **DB and natural attacks are derived from the rules:** the system's DB, so fire 22 and light 23; one size ladder for the attacks, Small 1d6 / 1d5. |
| B10-11 | **"Dragon Lore"** is the Dragon Lore specialist skill, however gained. |
| B10-12 | **A second companion:** one companion per kind (core C1). "One bond per lifetime" means one dragon. |
| B10-13 | **Losing a dragon:** death is automated: Soul Drain equal to its level, held by the bond; −2 on Refocus; 1 point back each 7 days of daily preparation. Separation is a GM toggle. Insanity and re-spending feats are the GM's. |
| B10-14 | **Release:** Inner Tempest 1.0.0 with Block 10, on TRG Prime 3.674.0. B8-9's three spell upgrades follow later as 1.0.x with their core block. |

**Interpretations 1 to 7 stand**, as written in the audit.

**Amended 28 October 2026 (after the live test of 1.0.0).** Graham: "Printed block for dragon db." B10-10's DB half is replaced: a dragon's DB is the printed block's, which is the system's Creature Codex rule for companions (half the level as an other bonus, class bonus − 2 as a status bonus): cold 19, earth 19, fire 23, light 24. The format line's "their class bonus as a status bonus to DB" adds nothing beyond it. B10-10's natural-attack half (one size ladder) stands.
